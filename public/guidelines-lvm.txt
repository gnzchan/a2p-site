A2P 10DLC COMPLIANCE GUIDELINE — LOW VOLUME MIXED (LVM)
================================================================================

CAMPAIGN TYPE
Low Volume Mixed. Sends BOTH transactional/customer care SMS AND marketing/
promotional SMS to opted-in users. Lower volume caps than Standard, less
aggressive carrier filtering, easier brand vetting.

REQUIRED PAGES
• Homepage - business info, address, phone
• Contact Page - PRIMARY opt-in destination (form with dual SMS consent)
• Privacy Policy - A2P section covering both transactional and marketing
• Terms of Service - SMS terms section covering both transactional and marketing

DO NOT INCLUDE
• Standalone SMS opt-in page. LVM submissions list /contact as the sole opt-in
  destination.

OPT-IN FORM REQUIREMENTS (on /contact)
• Fields: name, email, phone (validated), subject, message
• Three separate consent checkboxes, all unchecked by default:
  1. Transactional / customer care SMS consent
  2. Marketing / promotional SMS consent
  3. Terms of Service & Privacy Policy agreement
• Form submittable without any consent box checked (user can just send a message)
• Submit button text MUST read "Submit"

CONSENT LANGUAGE (LVM)

Transactional / Informational / Customer Care:
"By checking this box, you agree to receive transactional, informational, and
customer care text messages from [COMPANY] at the number provided. Message
frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out
and HELP for help."

Marketing / Promotional:
"By checking this box, you agree to receive marketing and promotional text
messages from [COMPANY] at the number provided. Message frequency may vary.
Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help.
Consent is not a condition of purchase."

Terms Consent:
"By checking, I accept Terms of Service & Privacy Policy."

• Must link to Terms and Privacy Policy
• Cannot bundle SMS consent with terms (kept as three separate checkboxes)
• Cannot bundle transactional consent with marketing consent (must be separate)

MANDATORY DISCLOSURES (each SMS consent checkbox)
• Message frequency: "varies" or "based on use case" or specific number
• Cost: "Message & data rates may apply"
• STOP: "Reply STOP to opt out"
• HELP: "Reply HELP for help"
• On marketing checkbox only: "Consent is not a condition of purchase."
• Links to Terms and Privacy Policy on the terms consent

BUSINESS INFORMATION (visible on site)
• Legal business name + type (LLC, Corp, etc.)
• Complete physical address (street, city, state, zip)
• Primary phone number (for HELP keyword)
• Business email address — must match site domain
• Display on: homepage, contact page, footer, legal docs

PRIVACY POLICY - REQUIRED SECTIONS
1. Information We Collect
   - SMS subscription data, separated by transactional vs marketing consent
   - Contact info, property details
2. SMS Text Message Service
   - Describe BOTH categories: transactional/customer care AND marketing/promotional
   - "Mobile information will NOT be shared with third parties for marketing"
   - Message frequency, cost disclosure
   - "Reply STOP to opt out" + "Text HELP for assistance"
3. Information Sharing
   - "We do not sell, trade, or transfer personal information to third parties
     for marketing purposes"
4. A2P 10DLC Compliance (dedicated section)
   - Compliance with A2P 10DLC and TCPA
   - Registered under Low Volume Mixed use case
   - Practices: separate explicit opt-in per message category, clear opt-out,
     transparent disclosures, proper registration
5. Contact Information
   - Full business details (name, address, phone, email)
6. Last Updated date

TERMS OF SERVICE - REQUIRED SECTIONS
1. SMS Text Message Service Terms
   - Both categories described
   - "By checking the transactional SMS consent box, you expressly consent to
     receive transactional, informational, and customer care SMS from [COMPANY].
     By checking the marketing SMS consent box, you expressly consent to receive
     marketing and promotional SMS from [COMPANY]. Consent to marketing is not
     a condition of any purchase or service."
   - Message frequency, "Message & data rates may apply"
   - "Carriers not liable for delayed/undelivered messages"
   - "Text STOP to opt out"
   - "Text HELP for support"
   - "You will receive confirmation when you opt out"
2. A2P 10DLC Compliance
   - "Our SMS services comply with A2P 10DLC and TCPA regulations and are
     registered under the Low Volume Mixed use case"
   - Practices: explicit consent required per category, clear opt-out, consent
     records maintained, messages only for stated purposes
3. Privacy and Data Protection
   - "We will never sell or share your mobile number with third parties for
     marketing purposes"
   - Reference Privacy Policy
4. Contact Information + Governing Law
5. Last Updated date

TECHNICAL IMPLEMENTATION
• Centralized config file with `useCase: "LOW_VOLUME"` flag
• Consent validation: log timestamp per checkbox checked
• All pages use HTTPS
• Consistent business info across all pages
• Header with nav to legal pages (NO link to /sms-optin — page does not exist)
• Footer with business info + legal links on every page

CONFIG STRUCTURE (LVM example)
{
  companyName: "Company LLC",

  address: { street, city, state, zip, country },
  phone: "555-123-4567",
  email: "support@company.com",
  smsBrand: "BrandName",
  useCase: "LOW_VOLUME",
  messageFrequency: "Varies based on your inquiry"
}

COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID
- Pre-checked consent boxes
- Bundling transactional and marketing consents into one checkbox
- Bundling SMS consent with terms agreement
- Missing "Consent is not a condition of purchase" on the marketing checkbox
- Submit button labelled anything other than "Submit"
- Including a standalone SMS opt-in page (LVM uses /contact only)
- Missing STOP/HELP keywords on either SMS consent
- Missing frequency/cost disclosure on either SMS consent
- Incomplete business address
- Missing legal doc links on the terms consent
- Inconsistent business info across pages
- Vague consent language

LAUNCH CHECKLIST (LVM)
Legal Docs:
- Privacy Policy with A2P section covering BOTH transactional and marketing
- Terms with SMS section covering BOTH transactional and marketing
- Current "Last Updated" dates
- Complete contact info in both
- "No third-party sharing" statements
- STOP/HELP in both

Forms (/contact only):
- Three checkboxes (transactional, marketing, terms) — all unchecked by default
- All disclosures visible
- "Consent is not a condition of purchase" on marketing
- Terms/Privacy links on terms consent
- Submit button text reads "Submit"
- No standalone /sms-optin route

Business Info:
- Legal name displayed
- Complete address on multiple pages
- Phone consistent everywhere
- Email visible, matches site domain
- Business type stated

Disclosures (each SMS consent):
- Frequency disclosed
- Cost disclosed
- STOP instructions
- HELP instructions
- Use case clearly described

Technical:
- HTTPS everywhere
- Centralized config used with useCase: "LOW_VOLUME"
- Business info consistent across pages
- Mobile responsive

REGULATORY COMPLIANCE
- A2P 10DLC (Application-to-Person 10-Digit Long Code)
- TCPA (Telephone Consumer Protection Act)
- Register business with TCR (The Campaign Registry)
- Complete 10DLC campaign registration as Low Volume Mixed use case
- Get carrier approval

KEY PRINCIPLES (LVM)
- Explicit opt-in only, per message category (transactional and marketing
  collected separately)
- Keep consent separate from other terms
- Marketing consent must include "Consent is not a condition of purchase"
- Use plain language
- Never pre-check consent boxes
- Display disclosures prominently
- Never share phone numbers with third parties for marketing
- Honor STOP requests immediately
- Store consent records with timestamps, including which categories were opted in
- Keep legal docs current
- Compliance is ongoing, not one-time
