Create a Next.js A2P 10DLC compliant SMS opt-in website for the Low Volume Mixed campaign use case with the following structure:

CAMPAIGN TYPE: Low Volume Mixed (LVM)
This site supports a campaign that sends BOTH transactional/customer care SMS AND marketing/promotional SMS to opted-in users. The form must collect SEPARATE consent for each category.

PAGES REQUIRED:
1. Homepage (/) - Display business info (company name, type, industry, full address, phone, email)
2. Contact (/contact) - Primary opt-in destination. Contact form with optional dual SMS opt-in (transactional checkbox + marketing checkbox + terms checkbox)
3. Privacy Policy (/privacy-policy) - With A2P compliance section covering both transactional and marketing
4. Terms of Service (/terms) - With SMS terms section covering both transactional and marketing

DO NOT INCLUDE: A standalone /sms-optin page. LVM submissions list /contact as the sole opt-in destination. If a /sms-optin route is generated, it must redirect to /contact.

CENTRALIZED CONFIG (lib/config.ts):
Export config object containing:
- companyName, businessType, industry
- address: {street, city, state, zip, country}
- phone, email, domain
- smsBrand
- useCase: "LOW_VOLUME"
- messageFrequency
- legal: {state, lastUpdated}

Helper functions:
- formatAddress(), formatFullAddress()
- getHelpText(), getStopText()
- getTransactionalConsentLanguage()
- getMarketingConsentLanguage()
- getTermsConsentLanguage()

CONTACT FORM (app/contact/page.tsx) - PRIMARY OPT-IN:
Fields: name (required), email (required), phone, subject (required), message (required).

THREE CONSENT CHECKBOXES inside a card labeled "Optional: Opt in to SMS updates" — all unchecked by default. Form is submittable without checking any of them (a user might just be sending a message without opting in).

Checkbox 1 - Transactional / Informational / Customer Care SMS:
"By checking this box, you agree to receive transactional, informational, and customer care text messages from [COMPANY] at the number provided. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help."

Checkbox 2 - Marketing / Promotional SMS:
"By checking this box, you agree to receive marketing and promotional text messages from [COMPANY] at the number provided. Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase."

Checkbox 3 - Terms & Privacy:
"By checking, I accept Terms of Service & Privacy Policy."
Include clickable links to /terms and /privacy-policy.

Display business contact info prominently (address, phone, email).
Submit button text: "Submit".

Additional disclosures section (rendered as a card below the form): Reply STOP to opt out at any time, Reply HELP for assistance, message frequency varies based on your inquiry, standard message and data rates may apply.

Validation: only the Terms & Privacy checkbox is required to submit. SMS consents (transactional and marketing) are optional — user may submit a contact message without opting in to either. Show error toast if Terms is unchecked: "Please accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy to continue."

Use Sonner for toast notifications:
- Loading: "Sending your message..."
- Success (no SMS opt-in): "Thank you for your message! We will get back to you soon."
- Success (with at least one SMS consent checked): "Thank you for your message! We will get back to you soon. You've also opted in to receive SMS updates."

PRIVACY POLICY (app/privacy-policy/page.tsx):
Sections:
1. Information We Collect - SMS subscription data (separated by transactional vs. marketing consent), contact info, property details
2. How We Use Your Information - cover both transactional purposes (inquiry follow-ups, scheduling, support responses) and promotional purposes (offers, discounts) gated on the user's specific consent selection
3. SMS Text Message Service - describe BOTH categories: transactional/customer care messages AND marketing/promotional messages. State that frequency varies, costs apply, "Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for assistance". Note that "Mobile information will NOT be shared with third parties for marketing"
4. Information Sharing - "We do not sell, trade, or transfer personal information to third parties for marketing"
5. A2P 10DLC Compliance - Compliance statement with A2P 10DLC and TCPA, list practices (separate explicit opt-in per message category, clear opt-out, transparent disclosures, proper registration as Low Volume Mixed)
6. Contact Information - Full business details
Display "Last Updated: [DATE]" at top.

```typescript
const handleSubmit = async (e: React.FormEvent) => {
  e.preventDefault();
  if (!formData.termsConsent) {
    toast.error("Please accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy to continue.");
    return;
  }
  // Process form. transactionalConsent and marketingConsent are optional —
  // record whichever the user checked, but do not block submission.
  toast.success("Thank you for your message! We will get back to you soon.");
```

KEY COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS (LVM-SPECIFIC):
- Two SEPARATE SMS consent checkboxes: (1) transactional/customer care, (2) marketing/promotional
- Each SMS checkbox carries its own STOP/HELP language and rate disclosure
- Marketing checkbox MUST include "Consent is not a condition of purchase."
- Terms & Privacy is a separate third checkbox
- Consent checkboxes never pre-checked
- All disclosures visible before consent
- Links to legal docs in terms consent checkbox
- Business info consistent across all pages
- Submit button text reads "Submit" (not "Send", "Sign up", etc.)
- "Message frequency varies" or specific frequency
- "Message & data rates may apply"
- "No third-party sharing" statements in Privacy and Terms
- Last Updated dates on legal docs
- NO standalone SMS opt-in page (opt-in lives only on /contact)

Use TypeScript, App Router structure, and follow Next.js 14+ best practices.
